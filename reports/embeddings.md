### What each model costs to run

Every model here runs locally on the CPU: no API key, no per-token bill, and no text leaves the machine. The price is a one-time download, memory for the weights, and CPU time for every chunk and query.

- **`minilm`** (`sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`): The usual sentence-transformers baseline: small and fast, but it reads only the first 256 tokens of a chunk and was trained on sentence pairs, not queries against passages. Weights: about 90.9 MB. At a million chunks the vectors alone take 1.5 GB, and re-embedding them at this machine's rate would take about 2 hours.
- **`bge-small`** (`BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5`): Trained for retrieval, same vector size as MiniLM, reads 512 tokens. Queries need the model card's instruction in front; documents do not. Weights: about 133.4 MB. At a million chunks the vectors alone take 1.5 GB, and re-embedding them at this machine's rate would take about 5 hours.
- **`bge-base`** (`BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5`): The same recipe as bge-small at three times the parameters and twice the vector size: more storage and compute per chunk and per query. Weights: about 437.9 MB. At a million chunks the vectors alone take 3.1 GB, and re-embedding them at this machine's rate would take about 13 hours.

That last number is the cost of changing models later: every stored vector belongs to one model's map, so a new model means re-embedding the whole corpus and rebuilding the index before the first query.
