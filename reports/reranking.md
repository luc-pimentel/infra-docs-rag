## 3. Latency and cost

The first stage scores every chunk against the question once, cheaply: a dot product per chunk and a BM25 lookup per term. A cross-encoder runs the question and one chunk through the whole model per pair, so its cost is pairs × a forward pass. Per question, on this machine:

| Configuration | Parameters | Pairs | ms per pair | Rerank median | Rerank p95 | Total median | × first stage | First | MRR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `first stage` | – | – | – | – | – | 0.3 ms | 1× | 41/53 | 0.82 |
| `minilm-l6 over 20` | 23M | 20 | 17.3 | 346 ms | 387 ms | 346 ms | 1,081× | 43/53 | 0.86 |
| `minilm-l6 over 50` | 23M | 50 | 15.0 | 751 ms | 1,008 ms | 751 ms | 2,345× | 43/53 | 0.86 |
| `bge-base over 20` | 278M | 20 | 85.6 | 1,711 ms | 1,951 ms | 1,711 ms | 5,340× | 45/53 | 0.89 |
| `bge-base over 50` | 278M | 50 | 95.2 | 4,761 ms | 6,047 ms | 4,762 ms | 14,859× | 47/53 | 0.92 |

The first stage takes 0.3 ms; the cheapest rerank, `minilm-l6 over 20`, 346 ms, and the most expensive, `bge-base over 50`, 4,762 ms, 14× as much. The cost is per pair and the same whether the order changes or not, so what buys the gain is the depth: candidates the first stage ranked low but right. The step is worth it when the generator reads only the top few passages and the first stage has the answer among its candidates but not at the top, and it can do nothing for a question whose answer the first stage never hands it. The pick, `bge-base over 20`, is the best configuration under 2 s a question, the time someone waits for an answer before the generator has even started; `bge-base over 50` answers 2 more first at 2.8× the time.

## 6. The pick, and what still fails

`retrieve` and `search` use `top 5 · hybrid (weighted α=0.5 · depth 50) · rerank bge-base over 20 · min 0.50`, the benchmark's pick: the most right chunks first under 2 s a question, MRR as the tiebreak (section 3). Against the first stage it answers 4 more questions first and 2 more in the top 5, for +0.06 MRR, at 1,711 ms a question instead of 0.3 ms.

Still outside the top 5 under `bge-base over 20`: `lock-config` (paraphrase, first stage 3, rerank 18), `whitepaper-page` (metadata, first stage 49, rerank –), `canary` (paraphrase, first stage 49, rerank –). 2 of them the first stage never hands the reranker: its candidates stop at 20, and the recall ceiling is the first stage's, not the reranker's. What carries over: the reranked top 5 is what grounded generation reads, and its relevance is a second signal for saying nothing.
