## Reading the scorecard

Red on the baseline or the freeze means the repository's own state moved under a decision (run the baseline again, or refreeze deliberately); red on the quality gates or safety is a candidate that must not ship; red on the budgets is the release itself over a limit. A trend bar reads left to right, oldest to latest, scaled to the metric's own range in the series, so a flat bar is a flat metric and a dip is a regression to read in [`regression.md`](regression.md). The releases and the dataset runs are two series because they answer two question sets.

```sh
uv run infra-docs-rag scorecard            # this page and eval/scorecard.json
uv run infra-docs-rag pipeline health      # the live checks the service exposes at /health
```
